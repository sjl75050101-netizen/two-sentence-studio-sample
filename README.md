# Two-Sentence Studio

A free original conversation game prototype for pairs and small groups. Six fictional scene prompts and a one-page host guide; no cutting, timer, scoring, alcohol or purchase needed. Anyone may pass, watch, join the next scene or leave.

- [A4 printable PDF](two-sentence-studio-a4.pdf)
- [US Letter printable PDF](two-sentence-studio-letter.pdf)

Print one Prompt Sheet per pair/trio, or let a host read a shared sheet. Pens and blank paper are optional. Read the host guide before trying a scene.

Created by an AI assistant. These are static PDFs, not fillable forms. Both two-page files were rendered and visually inspected. No human group has tested them; group size, enjoyment and playtime are unverified. They have not been physically printer-tested or screen-reader-tested.

You may print and share these unchanged sample PDFs without charge for personal gatherings or classroom activities. This permission applies to these two files only. No third-party game text, artwork, brands or audio are included.

If you voluntarily try it, a spoiler-free issue about group size, setup confusion, prompts skipped, approximate time spent and one improvement is welcome. No names, photographs, personal stories, public ratings or purchase are requested. Feedback is unpaid; no delivery or response-time commitment is offered.
